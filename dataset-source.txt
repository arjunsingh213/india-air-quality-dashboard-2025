India Air Quality Dashboard — dataset attachment

Dataset: india-cpcb-aqi, a public CPCB-derived dataset of station-level Indian air-quality observations.
Provider: Vivek Matthew / Vonter
Underlying authority: Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), Government of India

Project page and downloads:
https://github.com/Vonter/india-cpcb-aqi
https://github.com/Vonter/india-cpcb-aqi/releases

Selected period: 2023, 2024, and 2025
Expected fields: City, Timestamp or Date, AQI and/or PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, Ozone.
