India air quality · 2025

PM2.5 risk was persistent in Delhi NCR—but India’s pollution calendar is not one story.

A station-level analysis of 18.8 million CPCB records shows northern winter spikes dominate several high-risk cities, while places such as Surat and Thiruvananthapuram peak outside that pattern.

Headline takeawayAnnual averages alone hide two important differences: Delhi has frequent high-PM2.5 readings across the year, while Byrnihat’s ranking is more episode-driven; Surat’s July spike is a notable seasonal exception that warrants station-level validation.
